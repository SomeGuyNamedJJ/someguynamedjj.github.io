# Changes to Stance on Generative AI
Anyway, self promotion over, here's what I am actually here to talk about.

I have made some changes to my [Stance on Generative AI](/Stance-on-Generative-AI), mainly updating my point to be more adamently against the use of Generative AI, and to change how I disclose Generative AI.

## What's changing
**How I disclose Generative AI**: I'll be more obvious with how I disclose use of Generative AI (which I have not had to do yet) and where I state that it hasn't been used.

In fact, every page on this site now has a disclaimer stating that I have not used Generative AI. While I do intend on changing this on pages that do use Generative AI, currently none of those exist so this disclaimer always states that none was used.

**The wording of my stance**: I have reworded my stance to make it much more clear that I dislike Generative AI and directly oppose its use, also stating that it is impossible to avoid in some scenarios, which is why the disclosure system is in place.

**Use of links in YouTube disclosures**: For the time being, YouTube disclosures will no longer include links to my Stance on Generative AI, because I don't have enough of a 'channel history' to include links to my own website in video descriptions. As soon as I am allowed to use links, this change will be reverted.

## What's not changing
My actual stance on Generative AI. I still believe that it should be avoided as much as possible, and I will always strive to avoid its use.
