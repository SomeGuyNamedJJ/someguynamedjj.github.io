As I mentioned in [my last blog post](/Blog/Tons-of-site-updates-and-a-blog.md), I have now implemented RSS Feeds to my blog. Alongside that, I'm going to give some information on what I'm working on right now.

# RSS Feeds
My Blog now has an RSS Feed avaliable, which can be used to get updates through a compatible RSS Reader application. For more information, see [the guide](/RSS-Guide.md) or simply add `https://1nteresting.name/blog.xml` to your RSS Reader application.
