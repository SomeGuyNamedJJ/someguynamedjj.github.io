# About Me

Enough about my site, what about me?

I spend a lot of my time developing, whether that is a web app, game mod, or entire game. Most of my development projects are private (although some may release in the future), but I have made things such as Discord Bots, Minecraft mods, and simplistic games.

When developing games, I use [Godot](https://godotengine.org) as I find it to be an incredibly capable engine that can do everything I need without being dependent on a large company like Unity or Unreal.

Alongside programming, I quite enjoy taking photos, usually also editing them afterwards. I don't have a proper photo portfolio at the moment, but I might consider making one in the future.

I also play a lot of games, particularly Nintendo games and indie games. I particularly enjoy platformers, RPGs and Rythm games. I sometimes even [speedrun](https://to.1nteresting.name/sp) some.
